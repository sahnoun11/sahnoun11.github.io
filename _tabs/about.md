## What I do

**Purple Teaming.** I don't just hand over a pentest report and walk away — I sit with SOC teams to build detection rules out of the attacks I ran, closing the loop between offense and defense.

**Penetration Testing.** Web, API, infrastructure, Cloud (AWS/Azure), and full Active Directory compromise chains — recon → domain enum → local priv esc → lateral movement → domain admin → cross-trust attacks. OWASP Top 10 and OWASP ASVS as baseline methodology.

**Cyber Threat Intelligence.** Operational CTI using MISP, OpenCTI, OSINT, and dark web monitoring — turning raw indicators into reports SOC analysts can actually act on. See my [Cicada3301](/posts/cicada3301-ransomware-cti-report/) and [Stormous](/posts/stormous-ransomware-deep-analysis/) deep-dives, and my [CTI methodology guide](/posts/cyber-threat-intelligence-art-of-thinking-like-the-enemy/) for what that looks like in practice.

**Red Teaming.** Realistic adversary simulation mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, designed to stress-test detection and response — not just find a way in.

**AI / LLM Security.** My newest focus: prompt injection, RAG pipeline attacks, agentic tool abuse, MCP surfaces, and AI supply-chain risk. This is what I spoke about at [Black Hat MEA 2025](/posts/ai-threat-intelligence-blackhat-mea-2025/), and what my [AI Systems Security Specialist](/posts/ai-systems-security-specialist-series-overview/) series and [Web LLM Attacks](/posts/Web-LLM-Attack/) posts are built around.
